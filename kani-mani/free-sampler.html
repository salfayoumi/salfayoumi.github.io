Don’t Say It Like That!

Six everyday Palestinian Arabic expressions with meaning, pronunciation, mini-dialogues and tiny practice prompts. No previous Arabic needed.

01 · I’m going home
أنا رايِح عَالبيت.
Ana rāyeḥ ʿal-bēt.

Lana: أنا رايْحة عَالبيت. — Ana rāyḥa ʿal-bēt.

Noah: وينك؟ — Where are you?
Lana: أنا رايْحة عَالبيت. — I’m going home.
Your turn: Say “I’m going home” using the form that matches you.
02 · I want…
بِدّي قهوة، لو سمحت.
Biddi ahwe, law samaḥt.

I’d like a coffee, please.

Server: شو بدّك تشرب؟ — What would you like to drink?
Noah: بدي قهوة، لو سمحت. — I’d like a coffee, please.
Your turn: Replace قهوة with something you want.
03 · I don’t know
مِش عارِف / مِش عارْفة
Mish ʿāref / mish ʿārfe.

Male / female form: “I don’t know.”

Lana: وين المفتاح؟ — Where’s the key?
Noah: مش عارف. — I don’t know.
Your turn: Which form would Lana use?
04 · Really?
عَنْجَد؟
ʿAnjad?

Really? / Seriously?

Noah: السوق مسكّر اليوم. — The market is closed today.
Lana: عنجد؟ — Really?
Your turn: React naturally when someone tells you surprising news.
05 · No worries
وَلا يِهُمَّك.
Wala yhimmak.

No worries / don’t worry about it.

Noah: آسف، تأخّرت. — Sorry, I’m late.
Lana: ولا يهمك. — No worries.
Your turn: Use it after a small apology.
06 · Can you repeat?
مُمْكِن تْعيد؟
Mumkin tʿīd?

Can you repeat?

Local: [speaks quickly]
Noah: ممكن تعيد؟ — Can you repeat?
Your turn: This is a rescue phrase. Use it instead of leaving the conversation.
